About

Purchasing a home is one of life's most significant milestones, and it deserves exceptional service tailored to your unique needs. Village Lending was established to provide our customers with transparent, face-to-face guidance and a steadfast commitment to their best interests.

As an independent, locally focused resource, we partner with a wide network of wholesale lenders to do the shopping for you. Whether you are purchasing your first home or refinancing an existing property, we combine top-tier lending options with premium, personalized service to ensure you feel confident and informed every step of the way.

Loan Programs

There is no single best loan — there is the loan that fits your situation. As an independent broker, I shop these programs across my wholesale lender network to find the fit for you.

Conventional Loans

The standard path for buyers with solid credit — and often the simplest one.

  • Down payments as low as 3% for qualified buyers
  • No upfront mortgage insurance premium
  • Mortgage insurance can drop off once you’ve built enough equity
Talk to Dan about this program

FHA Loans

Government-backed loans designed for buyers with smaller down payments or less-than-perfect credit.

  • 3.5% down for qualified borrowers
  • More flexible credit guidelines
  • Mortgage insurance required
Talk to Dan about this program

VA Loans

An earned benefit for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses.

  • No down payment for qualified borrowers
  • No monthly mortgage insurance
  • A benefit you’ve earned — I’ll walk you through using it
Talk to Dan about this program

First-Time Homebuyer

Buying your first home comes with a lot of questions. This one is guidance first, paperwork second.

  • A step-by-step walkthrough of your first purchase
  • Down-payment assistance and state programs, including New Hampshire Housing (NH Housing Finance Authority)
  • Plain answers before you make an offer
Talk to Dan about this program

DSCR / Investor Loans

For real estate investors buying property to rent, not to live in.

  • Qualification based on the property’s rental income
  • Built for non-owner-occupied properties
  • A fit for landlords and portfolio builders
Talk to Dan about this program

HELOCs & Second Mortgages

Tap the equity you’ve built without refinancing your first mortgage.

  • Home equity lines of credit (HELOCs)
  • Fixed second mortgages
  • For renovations, debt consolidation, or other major expenses
Talk to Dan about this program

Refinance

Refinancing reshapes a mortgage around where you are now — a shorter term, no more mortgage insurance, or cash out for something big. We’ll run the numbers on a matched-term basis, so you can judge the real trade-off before deciding anything.

  • Shorten your loan term
  • Drop mortgage insurance
  • Pull cash out for major expenses
Talk to Dan about this program
